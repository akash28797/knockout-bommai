Wind them up

Two tin boxers, bolted to one platform. They can't step, only swing both fists and one leg. A jab scores 1, a cross with the other hand scores 2, a kick scores 3, blocking makes the other one bounce off, and leaning back makes punches miss. First to 25, or the most points after 60 seconds, wins.

Jabs are quick; the cross and the kick hit harder but are easier to see coming. Leaning back sways your upper body out of reach, so jabs and crosses miss and leave the attacker off balance, but a kick still catches you. Hit someone while they’re winding up and you land a counter for an extra point. A blocked attack makes the attacker recoil, so blocking opens a window to punish. Blocking drains your guard meter; empty it and your guard breaks for a moment. Esc pauses.
